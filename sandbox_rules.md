Please help us maintain a pleasant, welcoming environment for everyone.

- Be respectful of the region, staff, and other residents. Discrimination is not tolerated.
- No sex or adult activity, child avatars, or unattended bots.
- No broadly offensive, racist, religiously motivated, or politically motivated material.
- No unauthorised begging, tip jars, selling, spam, recruitment, advertising, scams, or fraudulent schemes. Scams may be abuse-reported immediately.
- No combat, magic, or weapons outside the designated combat area, reached through the teleporter system.
- Do not assault another resident without prior consent.
- Avoid items or activities that disrupt the region or other visitors.
- Do not enter, interfere with, or pass through occupied privacy areas.
- During busy periods, there is a 200 [Land Impact](https://community.secondlife.com/knowledgebase/english/calculating-land-impact-r273) limit; objects over the limit may be returned.
- Rez large builds in the sky or on sky platforms. Do not block teleporters, stores, adboards, privacy areas, or other public services.
- Take or delete your objects when you finish.

See [script limits](/sandbox/objects/scripting/limits) and [help points](/sandbox/help/help-points).

## If there is a problem

For an immediate issue on the region, use a [help point](/sandbox/help/help-points) or type **`/911 admin`** in nearby chat. Include relevant usernames and a short description of what happened. Screenshots can also help the management team understand an incident.
